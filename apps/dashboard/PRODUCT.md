This is a design-tool reading aid, **not product authority**. Resolve conflicts through
`../../docs/00_READ_FIRST.md`, Constitution, Decision Ledger, PRD, applicable contracts and ADRs.
Do not infer implementation, data rights or release acceptance from this summary.

## Platform

web

## Users

Product customer hypotheses remain construction digital teams, solution developers and SI/AI
teams; site personnel review results. For the 2026-10-20 demo-design task the Human selected
**general introduction / business stakeholders** as the first-view audience. This does not
replace the product's customer definition.

## Product Purpose

Accept externally produced spatial artifacts while preserving submitted bytes and evidence;
adjudicate use-specific conditions; expose reusable, reproducible observations and results.
See Constitution §제품 정체성 and PRD §1.1–1.1.2 for the authoritative wording.

## Operating Context

Desktop web for registration and mutation; mobile web for reading and review within the existing
below-1024px action restrictions. Console uses Core records; it does not create independent
measurement or authority. Global Home/Sites/Work and the site's five-area workspace remain
the incumbent structure, subject to accepted ADR-KSI-025/026 and ADR-KSI-039 boundaries.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Registration, file inspection, use suitability, computation, accuracy evaluation and human review
  are different facts. Never turn missing values into zero or successful measurements.
- Results/evidence are immutable additive records; current usability is a separate projection.
- No capture, flight, customer reconstruction or quantitative-accuracy/settlement promises.
- Preserve organization/site/area permissions, data rights and explicit AI-egress consent.
- A viewable public source does not establish product-format support or analysis eligibility.

## Evidence on Hand

Real, CC BY 4.0 Cambridge Coastcare / DataWA Floreat-f3 observations from 2021 and 2022:
two orthomosaics and two DSMs. These are **coastal**, not construction-site observations.
The public DSM pair has no observed vertical reference and uses EPSG:7850; the current
relative-volume product path blocks it. Generated calculation fixtures are separately synthetic.
See `../../docs/design/DEMO_EXPERIENCE_BRIEF.md` and its material manifest for exact identities.

## Product Principles

- Let a new viewer understand a place and its observations before asking them to register data.
- Let source/evidence distinguish actual observations, derived previews and synthetic examples.
- Explain what is known, what is withheld and why, without claiming all viewing is quantitative use.
- Make a complete task legible before expanding the feature set.

## Open Decisions

Demo audience and primary real-coastal-plus-separate-synthetic story are Human-confirmed; screen composition remains under review, not a deployed feature. Retention, external identity,
public exposure, third-party map use and real customer acceptance remain separately bounded.
