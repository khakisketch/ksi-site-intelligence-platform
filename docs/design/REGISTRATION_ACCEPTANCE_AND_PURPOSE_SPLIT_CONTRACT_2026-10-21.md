# Pair registration and display-purpose boundaries

Human-directed design. Product authority and implementation approval remain separate. See [domain invariants](../product/DOMAIN_INVARIANTS.md) and [analysis authority](API_FIRST_EARTHWORK_FIRST_REQUEST_CONTRACT.md).

- The server owns registration acceptance and the UI consumes the result. Start with verification of externally attested evidence. Automatic registration, new methods and thresholds require separate owner decisions.
- Bind the exact two material versions, transform and CRS, producer/method version, method evidence, error, valid area and intended comparison. A transform alone cannot produce `REGISTERED`.
- Check purposes separately: independent view, aligned overlay, difference colouring and quantitative volume. An unfit or missing registration permits only otherwise authorized independent viewing. Quantitative publication passes its own analysis gates.
- Display-tile reads, originals, analysis, external AI processing and dataset export have separate rights. Display authorization cannot confer downloads or AI-training rights. Review aggregate tile harvesting and reconstruction risks before defining limits.
- Ordinary exploration stays within a live grant without per-tile approvals. Calibrate finite aggregate limits from legitimate use. Provide a typed reason and recoverable route at a limit. No new permission, threshold, contract or rollout follows from this design.
