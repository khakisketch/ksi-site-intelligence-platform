# Product Roadmap and Market Positioning

## Position

KSI is not only mapping software, not only a drone autopilot and not only video AI. It is an edge-first site intelligence operating platform.

## Beachhead

Construction: safety, progress, spatial refresh and inspection share the same repeated-flight data loop.

## Expansion packs

Logistics yards, plants, energy, mining, infrastructure and emergency response.

## Competitive lens

- DJI provides aircraft, SDK, cloud integration and reconstruction tools.
- Pix4D/Drone mapping platforms lead photogrammetry and progress workflows.
- Skydio demonstrates adaptive autonomous structure capture.
- Flyability specializes in confined-space autonomy and mapping.

KSI differentiates with DJI-based edge autonomy, automatic equipment risk fields, operational event workflow, and a continuously updated 4D world model.
